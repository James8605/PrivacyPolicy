# Puppy Diary Privacy Policy

[简体中文 / Chinese version](https://github.com/James8605/PrivacyPolicy/blob/master/PuppyDiary_cn.md)

Effective date: September 14, 2026. Operator: Yang Zhang. Contact: zhangyang8605@gmail.com.

Puppy Diary helps you keep pet profiles, photos, care records, and reminders. This policy describes the current iPhone and iPad app.

## Your records

The app stores information you enter, including pet names, birthdays, breed, care notes, veterinary contact details, diary entries, weight records, reminders, and photos and videos you choose. Records are saved on your device. Language and selected-profile preferences are saved locally.

On supported devices signed into iCloud, the app uses Apple’s private CloudKit database to synchronize profiles, photos, videos, diary entries, and reminders between your devices. Apple operates iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. Synchronization depends on your iCloud account, available storage, and network connection.

The current app does not send your pet records to a developer-operated server. The developer does not have access to the contents of your private CloudKit database.

## Photos, videos, and notifications

The app uses Apple’s system photo picker to access photos and videos you select. Selected media become part of your saved records and can synchronize through iCloud.

If you grant notification permission, the app schedules local care and birthday alerts. You can change notification permissions in iOS Settings. CloudKit also uses Apple’s background notification services to support synchronization.

## Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDK. It does not sell your records or use them for targeted advertising. Apple may process service and diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings.

## External links and support

Care articles link to external educational websites. Visiting those websites is subject to their privacy policies.

If you contact support, the information you choose to send will be used to respond to your request. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information or private pet records. For questions about the handling or deletion of support correspondence, contact the email address above.

## Exports

You can export diary entries and reminders as CSV or text and share them using the system share sheet. The destination you choose receives the exported information. Photos and videos are not included in these exports.

## Managing and deleting information

You can edit or delete diary entries and manage profiles in the app. Deleting a puppy profile deletes its associated records and propagates deletions to synced iCloud data when synchronization succeeds. This action cannot be undone in the app. Removing the app from a device should not be relied upon to delete data already stored in iCloud or backups. Apple’s iCloud storage controls and backup policies also apply.

## Policy changes and contact

We will update this policy when the app’s practices change. For privacy questions, contact zhangyang8605@gmail.com.

# 狗狗日记隐私政策

[English / 英文版](https://github.com/James8605/PrivacyPolicy/blob/master/PuppyDiary.md)

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 14 日。运营者：Yang Zhang。联系邮箱：zhangyang8605@gmail.com。

狗狗日记帮助你保存宠物档案、照片、养护记录和提醒。本政策适用于当前的 iPhone 和 iPad 应用。

## 你的记录

应用保存你输入的信息，包括宠物姓名、生日、品种、养护备注、兽医联系方式、日记、体重记录、提醒，以及你选择的照片和视频。这些记录保存在你的设备上。语言和所选档案等偏好设置也保存在本地。

在已登录 iCloud 的受支持设备上，应用使用 Apple 的私人 CloudKit 数据库，在你的设备之间同步档案、照片、视频、日记和提醒。Apple 根据其自身的条款和[隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)运营 iCloud。同步取决于你的 iCloud 账户、可用存储空间和网络连接。

当前应用不会将你的宠物记录发送到开发者运营的服务器。开发者无法访问你的私人 CloudKit 数据库中的内容。

## 照片、视频和通知

应用使用 Apple 的系统照片选择器，访问你主动选择的照片和视频。所选媒体会成为已保存记录的一部分，并可通过 iCloud 同步。

如果你授予通知权限，应用会安排本地养护提醒和生日提醒。你可以在 iOS 系统设置中更改通知权限。CloudKit 也会使用 Apple 的后台通知服务来支持同步。

## 广告和分析

应用不包含广告、跟踪或第三方分析 SDK。应用不出售你的记录，也不会将其用于定向广告。Apple 可能根据其自身的政策及你的设备设置，处理服务和诊断信息。

## 外部链接和支持

养护文章包含外部教育网站的链接。访问这些网站时，适用相应网站的隐私政策。

如果你联系支持，你主动发送的信息将用于回复你的请求。请避免提供不必要的个人信息或私人宠物记录。如需咨询支持往来邮件的处理或删除事宜，请联系上述邮箱。

## 导出

你可以将日记和提醒导出为 CSV 或文本，并通过系统分享菜单进行分享。你选择的接收方或目标服务将收到导出的信息。导出内容不包含照片和视频。

## 管理和删除信息

你可以在应用中编辑或删除日记，并管理宠物档案。删除狗狗档案会删除其关联记录，并在同步成功后，将删除操作同步到 iCloud 中的数据。此操作无法在应用中撤销。请勿将卸载应用视为删除已保存在 iCloud 或备份中的数据的方法。Apple 的 iCloud 存储管理规则和备份政策也适用。

## 政策更新和联系

当应用的数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本政策。如有隐私问题，请联系 zhangyang8605@gmail.com。
