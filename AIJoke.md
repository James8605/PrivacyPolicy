# AI Joke Privacy Policy · AI 笑话隐私政策

Updated November 10, 2026 · 更新日期：2026 年 11 月 10 日

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

AI Joke does not require an app account. The information used to generate a joke depends on your version of the app and the engine you choose.

**Generating jokes.** In version 2.0, the default Apple Intelligence engine uses Apple's on-device foundation model. With that engine, the words you enter and the generated joke are processed on your device. Version 1.1 and earlier use a bundled on-device model and do not offer cloud generation. Older versions that keep a daily usage count store it on your device. Apple handles payments for App Store purchases.

**Optional cloud AI in version 2.0.** If you choose a cloud provider, the app sends your two words, selected style and generation instructions (including the requested language) directly to the provider or server you configure. It does not send your saved joke history. Before the first joke request to each provider and server address, including a test joke, the app explains the destination and asks for permission. Canceling sends no request. To stop sending requests to that provider, select a different engine. The same choices apply if you connect to your own local model server.

The service receives your request and generates the response. When you supply an API key, the service can associate the request with your provider account and may charge that account. Its own privacy policy and settings govern retention, use and deletion on that service. Deleting a joke in AI Joke does not delete the provider's copy. Avoid entering personal information you do not want the provider to receive. These requests do not pass through a developer-operated server.

**API keys.** The app stores keys in your device's Keychain and sends the configured key to the selected server to authenticate requests. Only enter a server address you trust. Standard cloud endpoints use HTTPS. A local server you configure may use HTTP. To delete a stored key, remove it in AI settings before uninstalling the app. Uninstalling alone may leave Keychain items on the device.

**History, speech and sharing.** Version 2.0 keeps recent jokes, favorites, preferences and cloud permissions locally. In History, you can delete a joke or clear the list, with the option to keep favorites. Uninstalling the app removes its local history and preferences. Read Aloud uses iOS speech synthesis. Copy places text on the system clipboard. Share creates a joke card for the destination you choose in the system share sheet.

**Advertising.** AI Joke displays Google AdMob ads. Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses (including to estimate approximate location), device identifiers, advertising data, product interactions, crash reports, performance data and other diagnostics. Google uses data for purposes including delivering and measuring advertising and analyzing SDK performance. AI Joke does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. The app does not send your joke prompts, saved jokes or provider keys to AdMob. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [its mobile ads data disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure).

**Contact.** For privacy questions or requests, visit [AI Joke support](https://zhangyang8605.top/support/6758673277). Updates to this policy will appear on this page.

## 简体中文

使用 AI 笑话无需注册应用账户。创作笑话时，哪些信息会被发送、由谁处理，取决于您使用的版本和选择的 AI 引擎。

**生成笑话。** 2.0 版默认使用 Apple 智能的设备端基础模型。选择此引擎时，输入的词语和生成的笑话都在设备上处理。1.1 及更早版本使用内置模型，不提供云端生成。旧版如记录每日使用次数，计数保存在设备上。App Store 内购的付款由 Apple 处理。

**2.0 版的可选云端 AI。** 选择云端提供商后，应用会把两个词语、所选风格及生成指令（包括输出语言）直接发送至您指定的提供商或服务器，不会附上已保存的笑话记录。首次向每个提供商及服务器地址发送请求前（包括测试笑话），应用会说明发送目的地并征求同意。取消后不会发送请求。若要停止向某个提供商发送请求，请切换引擎。连接您自己的本地模型服务器时，也会遵循上述流程。

所选服务接收请求并生成回复。如果使用 API 密钥，服务可能将请求关联到您的提供商账户，并按该账户的用量计费。服务如何使用、保存和删除数据，取决于其隐私政策和账户设置。在 AI 笑话中删除笑话不会删除提供商保存的副本。请勿输入您不希望对方收到的个人信息。请求不经过开发者运营的服务器。

**API 密钥。** 密钥保存在设备钥匙串中。应用会将设置的密钥发送到您选择的服务器以验证请求，请只填写您信任的服务器地址。预设的云端地址使用 HTTPS，您自行配置的本地服务器可能使用 HTTP。要删除密钥，请在卸载应用前从 AI 设置中移除。仅卸载应用可能不会删除钥匙串中的项目。

**历史、朗读与分享。** 2.0 版的最近笑话、收藏、偏好设置及云端权限保存在设备上。您可以在历史记录中删除单条笑话，也可以清空列表并选择是否保留收藏。卸载应用会清除其本地历史和偏好设置。朗读使用 iOS 语音合成。复制会将文字放入系统剪贴板。分享时，应用会生成笑话卡片，由您在系统分享面板中选择接收方。

**广告。** AI 笑话展示 Google AdMob 广告。Google 广告 SDK 可能处理 IP 地址（包括据此推算大致位置）、设备标识符、广告数据、产品互动、崩溃报告、性能数据及其他诊断信息。Google 将这些数据用于投放及衡量广告、分析 SDK 性能等用途。AI 笑话不会请求 Apple 的 App 跟踪透明度授权，也不会将您的笑话提示词、已保存的笑话或提供商密钥发送给 AdMob。请参阅 [Google 隐私政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=zh-CN)和[移动广告数据披露说明](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure?hl=zh-cn)。

**联系我们。** 如有隐私问题或相关请求，请访问 [AI 笑话支持页面](https://zhangyang8605.top/support/6758673277)。政策更新将在本页发布。
