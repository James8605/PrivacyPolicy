# AI Lab Privacy Policy

[简体中文](https://github.com/James8605/PrivacyPolicy/blob/master/AILab_cn.md)

Effective date: October 16, 2026. Developer: James Zhang. Contact: zhangyang8605@gmail.com.

AI Lab is an educational iPhone and iPad app about artificial intelligence. This policy describes the current app, also identified as llm-vis (bundle ID James-Zhang.llm-vis).

## Learning and model input

Lessons, visualizations, and the small language model run on your device. Text you enter into the model and local learning experiments is processed on your device and is not sent to the developer or a cloud AI provider. The app does not require an account or API key.

Lesson progress, reading position, completed checks, saved papers, read status, language, appearance, and any local support-code unlock are stored on your device. The app does not operate a developer account, cloud sync service, or developer database for these records. Device backups may include local app data according to your Apple settings.

## Purchases

Optional AI Lab Pro access is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase handled by Apple's StoreKit and App Store. The app checks Apple's verified transaction history on the device to determine access. The developer does not receive your payment card details or upload your individual transaction history to a developer server. Apple may provide sales reports and process account, payment, transaction, and diagnostic information under its own policies: [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Papers and external websites

Opening a paper or source link contacts its original third-party website. Those sites receive ordinary web-request information such as your IP address, browser information, and requested page, and may apply their own cookies and privacy policies. The in-app paper reader uses a non-persistent WebKit website data store; website data is not deliberately saved between reader sessions. Links opened in your external browser follow that browser's settings. AI Lab does not mirror papers or send your reading history to the developer.

## Advertising and analytics

The app contains no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics SDK, or developer-operated usage-reporting service. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Apple may process diagnostics according to your system settings and its policies.

## Support, retention, and deletion

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and whatever message or attachments you send. We use them to answer your request and retain them only as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. Avoid sending sensitive information. Contact zhangyang8605@gmail.com to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to required retention.

Delete the app to remove its local records from that device; offloaded apps and device backups may retain data according to your Apple settings. Deleting the app does not cancel or erase Apple's purchase records. You can restore eligible purchases using the same Apple Account. Contact individual external websites about information they process.

## Children and changes

AI Lab is a general educational app and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through an app account or analytics service. External websites have their own content and policies. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us to request deletion.

We may update this policy when the app's practices change and will update the effective date. The current version is available in this repository and through the app's Settings.
