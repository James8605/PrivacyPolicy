# Another Me (I, Simulator) Privacy Policy

[简体中文](AnotherMe_cn.md)

Effective date: October 16, 2026. Operator: James Zhang. Contact: zhangyang8605@gmail.com.

This policy covers Another Me for iPhone and iPad, listed on the App Store as I, Simulator. It is a fictional life simulation, with optional cloud-generated stories and conversations.

## Information on your device

Character profiles, fictional birthdays and relationships, choices, stories, memories, past lives and gameplay progress are saved in the app’s local storage. Reading, sound and provider preferences are stored locally. The app does not require a developer account and has no developer-operated save server or cross-device synchronization service. Device backups may contain app records, depending on your Apple backup settings.

Cloud API keys and an optional TypeSafe AI review key are stored in this device’s Keychain with device-only accessibility. They are not written to game saves or preferences. The selected service receives its key to authenticate requests. The developer does not receive your keys.

## Cloud stories and conversations

Cloud features require you to select a provider and supply your own API key. Presets include OpenAI, DeepSeek, Google Gemini, Mistral and OpenRouter; you may also configure a compatible HTTPS endpoint. When you use cloud narration, the app sends character details, simulated dates, relationships, relevant past events and memories, choices, draft stories and instructions to that provider to generate and review stories, reflections and endings. Character conversations also send the message you type, the current conversation and relevant fictional life history. Connection tests send a short test request. Requests may incur charges from the provider, separately from any app purchase.

The receiving provider can associate requests with your API account and receives network information such as your IP address. Its retention, security, training and international processing practices depend on its own policy and your account configuration. OpenRouter and other gateways may forward content to the model provider you select. Review the relevant policies before enabling a service: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/), [DeepSeek](https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html), [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [Mistral](https://legal.mistral.ai/terms/privacy-policy), and [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/privacy). For a custom endpoint, contact its operator.

An optional Jev story-review setting, off by default, sends character context, recent events, memories and draft choices to [TypeSafe AI](https://typesafe.ai/) when enabled and configured with a key. TypeSafe operates independently; review its current service terms and privacy information before enabling this setting.

Choose Hand-written stories in Settings → Story source to play the finite bundled collection without cloud generation. Provider failures do not automatically switch sources. Removing a key or switching sources stops future requests through that configuration; it does not erase requests already received by a provider. Chat messages are not saved as a conversation transcript by the app after you close Talk. Provider-side copies may remain under that provider’s policy. Do not enter sensitive personal information into character names or chat messages.

## Purchases, advertising and diagnostics

Apple handles payments for the optional one-time Pro unlock. The app uses StoreKit transaction information to verify access and restore purchases; it does not receive your payment card details. Pro does not include cloud-provider usage fees. Apple processes purchase and optional diagnostic information under [Apple’s privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) and your device settings.

The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics service, does not use an advertising identifier, and does not sell personal data or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Operational status messages may be recorded in local system logs; release builds do not intentionally log API keys or full cloud conversations.

## External content and support

Some historical illustrations load from credited external sources, such as NASA and ESO. Loading an illustration sends an ordinary image request, including network information, to its host. Opening source, privacy or support links also contacts the destination website under its own privacy practices.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use it to answer your request. Send only the details needed to investigate; never send API keys. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact zhangyang8605@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve an ongoing dispute.

## Managing your information

You can delete past lives in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app-container records; device or iCloud backups may retain earlier copies until managed through Apple’s backup controls. Keychain entries can persist after app deletion: remove provider keys in Settings → Story source before uninstalling, and revoke keys in the provider’s account dashboard if needed. Deleting local records does not delete information already sent to Apple, an AI provider, an image host, or support. Contact the relevant operator for its deletion and retention options.

The app is not designed as a children’s service. Stories can include adult life themes. The App Store age rating and each AI provider’s eligibility rules apply. We update this policy when our practices change. For privacy questions, contact zhangyang8605@gmail.com or visit [Support](https://james1895.xyz/support/6762003942).
